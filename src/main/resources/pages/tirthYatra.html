तीर्थ यात्रा (Pilgrimage)

Tirth Yatra is a spiritual journey program organized by Jain Sangh Pune to take devotees to sacred pilgrimage sites. These pilgrimages strengthen faith and connect the community with Jain heritage and holy sites.

The pilgrimage program includes:

  • Organized trips to major Jain pilgrimage destinations
  • Guided spiritual tours with knowledgeable mentors
  • Community bonding and shared spiritual experiences
  • Educational sessions about pilgrimage sites

Through these sacred journeys, devotees deepen their connection with their faith and gain spiritual growth while exploring important Jain heritage sites.
