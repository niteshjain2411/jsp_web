संस्कार शिविर (Character Development Camp)

Sanskar Shivir is a transformative program designed to instill positive values and character development in the youth of our community. These camps focus on building moral strength and ethical awareness grounded in Jain principles.

The program encompasses:

  • Workshops on Jain ethics and values
  • Mental and physical wellness activities
  • Mentorship from spiritual leaders
  • Team-building exercises promoting community bonds

Through Sanskar Shivir, we aim to shape future generations who are not only educated but also morally conscious and spiritually aware.
