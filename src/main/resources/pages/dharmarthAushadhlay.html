धर्मार्थ औषधालय (Charitable Medical Clinic)

Dharmarth Aushadhlay is a charitable medical facility established by Jain Sangh Pune to provide healthcare services to underserved and economically disadvantaged sections of the community. This initiative embodies the Jain principle of charity and compassion.

The clinic offers:

  • Free or subsidized medical consultations
  • Preventive healthcare and wellness programs
  • Medical assistance for needy patients
  • Health awareness and education sessions

Through this compassionate initiative, we ensure that healthcare is accessible to all, regardless of their economic status, reflecting the core Jain values of service and support.
