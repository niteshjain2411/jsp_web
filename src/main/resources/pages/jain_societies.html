1. Gyanoday Digambar Jain Society, Sus

The Gyanoday Digambar Jain Society based in Sus, Pune, serves as an active neighborhood hub for local Jain residents. The community prides itself on maintaining values of mutual coordination, spiritual discourses, and preserving customary Jain lifestyle choices inside residential spaces.

Society Initiatives & Social Fabric:
  • Community Assemblies: Hosts regular interactive neighborhood meetups to organize local social support systems.
  • Cultural Gatherings: Actively celebrates seasonal Jain community events and religious functions across the annual calendar.
  • Ethics Frameworks: Conducts targeted group sessions focusing on spiritual harmony and traditional values for families.

2. Veeroday 1 Digambar Jain Society, Kharadi

Veeroday 1 is a thriving Digambar Jain collective located in the eastern IT hub of Kharadi, Pune. Built to satisfy the profound spiritual aspirations of working professionals and local Jain families, this society represents modern urban living deeply anchored in timeless Jain ethics.

Key Highlights & Community Functions:
  • Daily Jinendra Archana: Provisioned space for safe, disciplined daily Abhishek and morning path options.
  • Parv Celebrations: Grand annual layout celebrations for Das Lakshana Parv and Mahavir Janma Kalyanak.
  • Swadhyay Path: Regular weekly scriptural discourses arranged for both elders and young IT youth.

3. Veeroday 2 Digambar Jain Society, Kharadi

📍 Location Details

Veeroday 2 Extension
EON Free Zone Road, Kharadi,
Pune - 411014, Maharashtra, India

Following the vibrant expansion of the community in Kharadi, Veeroday 2 was set up as an extension center to comfortably serve the increasing local Digambar Jain families. It operates in perfect unison with Veeroday 1 to ensure standard ethical resources reach every household.

Features & Core Pillars:
  • Sanskar Pathshala: Specialized moral classes conducted on weekends to educate children on continuous vegetarianism, basic stotras, and non-violence (Ahimsa).
  • Mahila Mandal Forums: Active women's group dedicated to managing organic cooking resources, festival fast management, and local social help drives.
  • Spiritual Inclusivity: Features a serene dhyana center welcoming workers looking for silent introspection after regular office shifts.

General Visiting Information & Amenities

  • Sus and Kharadi locations both provide dedicated community support areas easily accessible via major Pune city bypass highways.
  • Ample designated vehicle parking is provided at all society campuses for seamless festival turnouts.
  • Clean, thoroughly maintained spaces dedicated entirely to silent spiritual reading and meditation.
