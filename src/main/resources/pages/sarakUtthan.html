सराक उत्थान (Welfare Enhancement)

Sarak Utthan is an initiative dedicated to the holistic welfare and upliftment of the Jain community members. This program addresses the social, economic, and spiritual well-being of individuals and families.

The initiative focuses on:

  • Financial assistance for underprivileged community members
  • Healthcare support and awareness programs
  • Educational assistance for deserving students
  • Community development projects

Through Sarak Utthan, Jain Sangh Pune ensures that all members of the community have access to essential services and opportunities for a better quality of life.
