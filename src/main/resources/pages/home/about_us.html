About Jain Sangh Pune(JSP)

Since 2007, JSP has been serving the Jain community of Pune through spiritual guidance, social support, and community-led development programs. We combine tradition with a professional approach to deliver measurable impact.

Established on Mahavir Jayanti 2007 at Manikbag Jain Temple, JSP is a community trust founded by engaged professionals to preserve Jain culture, offer spiritual support, and provide social services for those in need.

We work across spiritual, educational and humanitarian activities — from organizing religious events and pilgrimages to running scholarship programs and medical camps — always with an emphasis on dignity and transparency.

⚖️ Registered Public Trust (E-6048/PUNE)

Our Vision

To nurture a compassionate, informed and resilient Jain community that upholds its values while positively contributing to wider society.

Our Mission

Enable individuals and families through spiritual guidance, education, healthcare, and targeted social assistance — driven by accountability and community participation.
