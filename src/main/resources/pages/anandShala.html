आनंदशाला (Play-based Jain Learning)

AnandShala is an innovative initiative by Jain Sangh Pune that teaches Jain principles and values through fun and interactive games. This program makes spiritual education engaging and memorable for children.

The AnandShala program includes:

  • Interactive games and activities based on Jain philosophy
  • Storytelling sessions featuring Jain saints and principles
  • Character development through play-based learning
  • Community engagement for children and families

By combining education with entertainment, AnandShala ensures that children develop a strong connection with their faith while enjoying the learning process.
