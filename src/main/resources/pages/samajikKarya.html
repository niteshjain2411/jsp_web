सामाजिक कार्य (Social Service)

Samajik Karya represents Jain Sangh Pune's commitment to social welfare and community development. This initiative encompasses various programs designed to address social issues and improve the quality of life for all community members.

The social service program includes:

  • Community welfare drives and relief programs
  • Support for marginalized and vulnerable populations
  • Environmental conservation initiatives
  • Disaster relief and emergency assistance

Through Samajik Karya, Jain Sangh Pune actively participates in social development and upholds the Jain principle of serving humanity while protecting the environment and promoting social justice.
