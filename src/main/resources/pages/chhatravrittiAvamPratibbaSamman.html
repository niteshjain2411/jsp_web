छात्रवृत्ति एवं प्रतिभा सम्मान (Scholarships & Talent Recognition)

This initiative is dedicated to recognizing and nurturing talent within the Jain community while supporting deserving students through scholarships and financial assistance. We believe that education is a powerful tool for personal and community development.

The program encompasses:

  • Merit-based scholarships for deserving students
  • Financial assistance for underprivileged talented students
  • Recognition and felicitation of achievers
  • Mentorship and guidance programs

By investing in education and recognizing talent, Jain Sangh Pune contributes to the development of capable individuals who will lead and serve the community in the future.
