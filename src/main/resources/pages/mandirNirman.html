श्री 1008 नेमिनाथ दिगंबर जैन मंदिर

Shri 1008 Neminath Digamber Jain Mandir, located in the Gyanoday Digambar Jain Society at Sus, Pune, is a sacred temple dedicated to Lord Neminath, the 22nd Tirthankara. This beautiful temple serves as a spiritual hub for the Jain community, offering a place for prayer, meditation, and religious ceremonies. The temple stands as a testament to the deep faith and commitment of the devotees in preserving and promoting Jainism in the region.

About the Temple

The temple is architecturally designed following traditional Jain principles and serves as a peaceful sanctuary for devotees. It is known for its serene atmosphere, well-maintained premises, and community activities. The temple hosts regular religious ceremonies, festivals, and cultural events celebrating Jain traditions throughout the year.

संत निवास - विद्या शान्ति निलय

Jain Sangh Pune is developing a Sant Niwas (Residence for Saints) named Vidya Shanti Nilay within the temple premises. This residence will provide a peaceful and conducive environment for Jain saadhu to stay, meditate, and engage in spiritual practices. The Sant Niwas will be equipped with basic amenities and facilities to support the spiritual needs of the residents, fostering a deeper connection with the temple and its activities.
