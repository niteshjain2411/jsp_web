जीर्णोद्धार (Heritage Restoration)

Jirnodhar is an initiative dedicated to the restoration and preservation of ancient and historical religious structures. This program ensures that our precious heritage sites are maintained for future generations while preserving their architectural and spiritual significance.

The restoration project includes:

  • Restoration of aging temples and religious structures
  • Preservation of historical artifacts and monuments
  • Architectural documentation and research
  • Community participation in heritage conservation

Through Jirnodhar, we honor the legacies of our ancestors and protect the physical manifestations of Jain culture and spirituality for posterity.
