संत निवास निर्माण (Monk's Residence Construction)

Jain Sangh Pune is committed to providing comfortable and dignified living spaces for monks and nuns who dedicate their lives to spiritual practice. Our Sant Nivas (monk's residence) construction initiative creates facilities that support their ascetic lifestyle.

This initiative focuses on:

  • Building residential facilities for monks and nuns
  • Providing meditation and prayer spaces
  • Ensuring basic amenities for spiritual practitioners
  • Supporting the monastic community in their spiritual pursuit

These sanctuaries provide a peaceful environment where monks and nuns can continue their spiritual journey and serve as beacons of enlightenment for the community.
