About JSP

Established on Mahavir Jayanti 2007 at Manikbag Jain Temple, Jain Sangh Pune (JSP) is a religious and social trust founded by highly educated young professionals.

Our mission is to support all Jains residing in or visiting Pune in practicing their faith, providing comprehensive assistance and bringing the community together on a single platform.


JSP is officially registered under the Bombay Public Trust Act, 1950 (Reg No: E-6048/PUNE).
